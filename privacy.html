Privacy policy

How REESTV collects, uses and protects personal information through callready.com.au.

Who we are

REESTV (ABN 81 326 703 742) is based in Melbourne, Victoria, and operates callready.com.au and the CallReady service. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean REESTV.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you contact us, book a demo or ask about a pilot: your name, email address, phone number, organisation and role, and anything you choose to tell us.

When you use the voice assistant on this site: a recording and transcript of the conversation, and the name you give the assistant.

When you browse the site: basic technical information such as your browser, device and pages visited. [Remove or update this if no analytics are used.]

How we use it

To answer your questions, arrange demos and pilots, and follow up on your enquiry.

To review and improve the voice assistant and the site.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

The voice assistant

The assistant is an AI voice agent. Your conversation is processed by our voice provider, ElevenLabs, so the assistant can listen and reply.

Recordings and transcripts are kept for up to [30] days so we can check answers and improve them, then deleted.

Please don’t share health, patient or other sensitive information with the assistant.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the site: ElevenLabs (voice assistant), Cloudflare (security and delivery), [hosting provider], [email and booking tools] and [analytics provider].

Some of these providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

CallReady client deployments

This policy covers the website. Information about sales reps and users inside a client’s CallReady deployment is handled under our agreement with that client, and the client’s own privacy policy.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

We keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires, and then delete or de-identify it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or make a complaint, by emailing matthew@reestv.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page, with the date it took effect.
