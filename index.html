Built for pharma

Ready for Medical, Compliance and IT

Built by someone with 25+ years in Australian pharma, so the review questions are answered from the start.

Your content, your approval

  • Doctors respond using your approved product information and key messages.
  • Your Medical and Compliance teams review all doctor instructions before roll-out.
  • Doctors and scenarios are built for your therapeutic area.
  • The doctors are fictional, for practice only. No medical advice.

Your data, kept separate

  • Each client’s data is kept separate, with a dedicated database for early clients.
  • Reps sign in, and results go only to that rep and agreed reporting.
  • Transcripts and results are kept for feedback. Call audio is not kept.
  • We work with your IT team on hosting, security and access.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it voice or text?

Voice. Reps speak and the doctor speaks back in real time, like a real conversation.

How long is a practice call?

Usually a few minutes, much like a real detail. Reps can repeat it as often as they like.

Can reps practise with several doctors?

Yes, in a future release. Group scenarios, such as a practice lunch meeting, are on the way.

Can managers see how the team is going?

Reporting for managers and trainers is agreed with you during setup.

Can Medical review the content?

Yes, and we encourage it. All doctor instructions are provided before roll-out.

How much does it cost?

It depends on products, doctors and users. Matthew will put together a proposal.
